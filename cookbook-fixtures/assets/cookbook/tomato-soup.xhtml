Roasted tomato soup with white beans and basil

A synthetic review fixture with a longer recipe, alternative quantities, and source evidence.

4 servings

Ingredients

2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

1 large yellow onion, finely chopped

3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced

2 carrots, peeled and diced

2 tbsp tomato paste

2 × 400 g cans whole tomatoes

500 ml vegetable stock

1 × 400 g can white beans, drained

1 tsp dried oregano

1/2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes, optional

1 handful fresh basil leaves, torn

salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Instructions

Heat the olive oil in a heavy pot over medium heat. Add the onion and carrots and cook for 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened.

Stir in the garlic, tomato paste, oregano, and red pepper flakes. Cook for another 2 minutes until the tomato paste darkens slightly.

Add the tomatoes and vegetable stock. Break up the tomatoes with a spoon, bring to a gentle simmer, and cook uncovered for 25 minutes.

Add the drained white beans and simmer for 5 minutes more. For a thicker soup, mash a few of the beans against the side of the pot.

Taste and season with salt and pepper. Stir in the basil just before serving.
