Graham Cracker Crust

Season: Any | Active Time: 10 minutes | Total Time: 25 minutes | Difficulty: 1 (Very Easy)

A press-in crust is the fastest route to a pie, and the only real variable is how finely you grind the crumbs. Sandy is right; powdery is too far.

Makes one 9-inch crust

Special Equipment: food processor

9 ounces (255g) graham crackers, about 18 sheets

3 tablespoons (38g) sugar

¼ teaspoon Diamond Crystal kosher salt

6 tablespoons (85g) unsalted butter, melted

1 large egg white

Grind: Pulse the graham crackers with the sugar and salt in a food processor until sandy.

Press: Drizzle in the butter and egg white, pulse to combine, and press the mixture firmly up the sides and across the bottom of a 9-inch pie plate.

Bake: Bake at 350°F until the edges are set and lightly browned, 12 to 15 minutes. Cool completely before filling.

Speculoos Variation

Substitute 200g speculoos cookies for the graham crackers and omit the sugar.

All-Butter Pie Dough

Season: Any | Active Time: 20 minutes | Total Time: 1 hour, 20 minutes | Difficulty: 2 (Easy)

Leave the butter in pieces you can still see. Those sheets of butter are what turn into steam in the oven and push the layers apart.

Makes enough for one double-crust 9-inch pie

2½ cups (325g) all-purpose flour

1 tablespoon (13g) sugar

1 teaspoon Diamond Crystal kosher salt

1 cup (226g) cold unsalted butter, cut into ½-inch cubes

Combine the dry: Whisk the flour, sugar, and salt together in a large bowl.

Cut in the butter: Toss the butter in the flour and flatten each cube between your fingers until the pieces are the size of playing cards.

Add water: Drizzle in ½ cup ice water a little at a time, tossing, until the dough holds together when squeezed.

Chill: Divide in two, press each half into a disk, wrap, and refrigerate at least 1 hour.

Do-Ahead: The dough can be refrigerated for 3 days or frozen for 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator before rolling.
