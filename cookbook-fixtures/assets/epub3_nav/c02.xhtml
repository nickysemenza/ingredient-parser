Cranberry-Pomegranate Mousse Pie

Season: Fall / Winter | Active Time: 1 hour | Total Time: 1 hour, plus 4 hours for chilling | Difficulty: 2 (Easy)

Cranberries are loud and pomegranate is sweet-tart, and whipping the two of them into a mousse rounds off every hard edge without losing the color. The filling sets with gelatin rather than eggs, so it slices cleanly straight from the refrigerator.

Serves 8

Special Equipment: 9-inch pie plate

2 cups (200g) fresh or frozen cranberries

1½ cups (360g) pomegranate juice①

¾ cup (150g) sugar

2 tablespoons (16g) cornstarch

¼ teaspoon Diamond Crystal kosher salt

2 teaspoons (6g) powdered gelatin

3 tablespoons (45g) cold water

1 cup (240g) heavy cream, cold

2 tablespoons (25g) sugar

Graham Cracker Crust, Speculoos Variation (this page), fully baked

Cook the fruit: Combine the cranberries, pomegranate juice, sugar, and salt in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Simmer until every cranberry has burst, about 10 minutes.

Thicken: Whisk the cornstarch with a spoonful of the hot liquid, stir it back into the pan, and cook 2 minutes more, until glossy.

Bloom the gelatin: Sprinkle the gelatin over the cold water and let it stand 5 minutes, then stir it into the hot fruit until fully dissolved.

Purée and chill: Blend the mixture smooth, press it through a fine-mesh sieve, and refrigerate until it is cool but not set, about 45 minutes.

Whip and fold: Whip the cream with the 2 tablespoons sugar to soft peaks, then fold it into the cooled fruit base in three additions.

Fill and set: Scrape the mousse into the baked crust, smooth the top, and refrigerate at least 4 hours before slicing.

Sour Cherry Pie

Season: Summer | Active Time: 45 minutes | Total Time: 2 hours | Difficulty: 3 (Moderate)

Sour cherries are in season for about ten minutes a year, and this is what they are for. If you only have frozen, do not thaw them first; they release far too much liquid.

Serves 8

2 pounds (900g) fresh or frozen sour cherries, pitted

1 cup (200g) sugar

3 tablespoons (24g) cornstarch

1 tablespoon (15g) lemon juice

¼ teaspoon almond extract

¼ teaspoon Diamond Crystal kosher salt

1 recipe All-Butter Pie Dough, chilled

1 large egg, beaten, for egg wash

Macerate: Toss the cherries with the sugar, cornstarch, lemon juice, almond extract, and salt. Let stand 20 minutes.

Roll the bottom: Roll half the dough into a 13-inch round and line a 9-inch pie plate. Chill while you roll the top.

Fill and lattice: Scrape in the filling, weave a lattice from the remaining dough, and crimp the edges. Brush with egg wash.

Bake: Bake at 425°F for 20 minutes, then reduce to 375°F and bake until the juices bubble thickly at the center, 45 to 55 minutes more.

Cool completely: Cool on a rack at least 4 hours. A warm fruit pie will not slice.
