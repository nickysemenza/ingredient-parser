serves 4 to 8

Roasting mushrooms hard and fast drives off their water and concentrates everything that is left. The vinegar and onion go on while the pan is still hot, so the marinade soaks in instead of sitting on top.

2 pounds fresh mushrooms, such as maitake, oyster, or trumpet, sliced

¼ cup olive oil, plus more for drizzling

Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

1 small white onion or shallot, thinly sliced into rings

¼ cup white wine vinegar

Fresh herbs, such as parsley, chives, or cilantro (optional)

1 Preheat the oven to 425°F. Toss the mushrooms with the olive oil on a rimmed baking sheet and season generously with salt and pepper.

2 Roast, tossing once halfway through, until the edges are deeply browned and crisp, 25 to 30 minutes.

3 Scatter the onion over the hot mushrooms and douse everything with the vinegar. Let sit at least 20 minutes, then taste and season again. Top with herbs and a little more olive oil before serving.

DO AHEAD Mushrooms can be roasted and marinated 2 days ahead. Cover and chill, then bring to room temperature before serving.

NOTE Any mushroom works here, but the frilly ones catch the most marinade.
