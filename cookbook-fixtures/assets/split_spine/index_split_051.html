diy martini bar

Nobody wants to be the person making drinks all night, which is why the bar goes on a tray and the tray goes somewhere other than the kitchen. Gin, vermouth, a bowl of ice, and a jar of olives is the whole setup.

Keep the glasses in the freezer and the vermouth in the refrigerator. Warm vermouth is the single most common way a perfectly good martini goes wrong.

TO MAKE Stir 2 ounces gin with ½ ounce dry vermouth and plenty of ice until the outside of the mixing glass frosts, then strain into a chilled glass and garnish with an olive or a twist.
