serves 4

The potatoes go in first and get a head start, so by the time the chicken skin is the color you want, everything underneath has gone crisp in the rendered fat.

2 pounds small waxy potatoes, halved

3 tablespoons olive oil

Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs

1 lemon, thinly sliced, seeds removed

4 sprigs thyme

1 cup Tangy Roasted Mushrooms (see this page)

Flaky sea salt, for serving

1 Preheat the oven to 450°F. Toss the potatoes with the olive oil on a rimmed baking sheet, season with salt and pepper, and roast 15 minutes.

2 Season the chicken all over and nestle it among the potatoes, skin side up, along with the lemon slices and thyme.

3 Roast until the skin is browned and the chicken is cooked through, 30 to 35 minutes.

4 Scatter the roasted mushrooms over the hot pan, let them warm through for a minute, and finish with flaky salt.

DO AHEAD Potatoes can be roasted several hours ahead and left on the sheet at room temperature.
