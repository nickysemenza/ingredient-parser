SOURCES AND RESOURCES

Coarsely ground polenta is sold as polenta, granturco, or simply corn grits, and the three are not reliably the same thing. What matters is the grind: you should be able to see individual pieces of grain, not a powder.

Stone-ground cornmeal is perishable and should smell sweet. If it smells like nothing at all it is old, and no amount of salt or butter will fix the finished dish.

Mail-order mills will sell a pound at a time and ship it within a few days of milling, which is worth the trouble if your market only carries the fine grind.
