CORN

Cut the kernels off raw and scrape the cobs afterward with the back of the knife~the milky liquid that comes off is the best part and never survives boiling.

FOR ABOUT 2 CUPS:

4 small ears very fresh corn, shucked

1 tablespoon unsalted butter

Salt, to taste

Cut the kernels from the cobs, scrape the cobs over the bowl, and warm everything in the butter over low heat for 2 minutes. Season and use at once.
